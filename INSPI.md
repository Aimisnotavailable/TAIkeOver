*Plague Inc.* is a real-time strategy simulation where you play as a pathogen with the singular goal of infecting and ultimately wiping out humanity. The core tension is a race against the clock: you must spread globally and evolve your disease strategically before the world's governments develop and distribute a cure.

### 🧬 Core Gameplay Loop

The game unfolds in real-time (days pass in seconds), with the option to pause or fast-forward (fast-forwarding is a paid feature).

1.  **Setup:** You begin by naming your pathogen, selecting its **starting country** (Patient Zero), and optionally choosing a set of **genes** (unlockable modifiers).
2.  **Spread & Collect:** As your infection spreads, you collect **DNA Points** by tapping red and orange bubbles that appear on the map. Points also accrue automatically as more people are infected or die.
3.  **Evolve:** Spend DNA Points to evolve your pathogen across three main categories: **Transmission**, **Symptoms**, and **Abilities**. These upgrades affect your three core stats:
    *   **Infectivity (Biohazard Icon):** How fast the disease spreads within and between countries.
    *   **Severity (Yellow Bar):** How "bad" the disease appears, which increases cure research speed but also grants more DNA Points.
    *   **Lethality (Skull Icon):** How deadly the disease is.
4.  **Countermeasures:** Once a country detects your plague, global cure research begins. You lose if a cure is completed and distributed before you kill everyone.

### 🧪 Evolution Categories

Your DNA Points are spent in three distinct tabs:

*   **Transmission:** These traits boost how quickly and easily the disease spreads between populations and across borders. Key examples include **Air** (travel on dust particles), **Water** (survive in water), **Bird** (birds become carriers), and **Rodent** (flees become carriers). Transmissions do not typically increase Severity or Lethality.
*   **Symptoms:** Mutations that raise Infectivity, Severity, and (in high-tier cases) Lethality. Early symptoms like **Coughing** and **Sneezing** are good for stealthy spread. Late-game symptoms like **Total Organ Failure**, **Hemorrhagic Shock**, and **Necrosis** are the most deadly.
*   **Abilities:** Traits that grant resistances or special defenses. Common examples are **Climate Resistance** (survive hot/cold climates), **Drug Resistance** (combat medicine), and **Interfere with Treatment** (slow cure research). Some abilities, like **Environmental Hardening**, also boost Infectivity without raising Severity.

### 🧬 Genetic Code (Genes)

Before starting a game, you can equip genes (unlocked through gameplay) to modify your strategy from the outset. They fall into categories like DNA, Mutation, Travel, Environment, and Evolution. Notable examples include:

*   **ATP Boost:** Get 8 bonus DNA at the beginning.
*   **Cytochrome Surge:** Get more DNA from popping orange DNA bubbles.
*   **Metabolic Jump:** Get more DNA from popping red biohazard bubbles.
*   **Catalytic Switch:** Get bonus DNA from popping blue cure bubbles.
*   **Metabolic Hijack:** Orange and red bubbles are automatically popped.

Special plagues have their own unique gene sets (e.g., Necroa Virus genes replace Evolution genes).

### ⚙️ Difficulty & Game Modes

The game features four difficulty settings that drastically alter the world's response:

*   **Casual:** The easy mode. People don't wash their hands, doctors don't work, and sick people are given hugs (increasing Infectivity once spotted).
*   **Normal:** The default. 67.3% of people wash their hands, doctors work 3 days a week, and sick people are ignored.
*   **Brutal:** A hard challenge. Compulsive hand washing, doctors never go home, and sick people are locked in prison (greatly reducing infection rates once spotted).
*   **Mega Brutal:** The "very hard" mode. Introduces **Genetic Drift**, where symptom costs become more expensive as more people are infected, and the plague can be randomly detected through medical check-ups.

**Game Modes:**
*   **Normal:** The standard, untimed mode where you simply try to wipe out humanity.
*   **Speed Run:** A mode where the goal is to destroy humanity as quickly as possible.
*   **Scenario:** Custom or developer-created scenarios with unique rules and goals.
*   **Cure Mode (The Cure):** A complete role reversal. You play as a global health expert trying to develop a cure and stop a pandemic before **Authority** (public trust) reaches zero. You invest resources into disease research, lockdowns, port closures, and border restrictions.
*   **Outbreak Mode / Versus / Co-Op:** Competitive and cooperative multiplayer variants where players race to infect the world or work together against it.

### 🛠️ Scenario Creator

A separate app, **Plague Inc: Scenario Creator**, allows players to design their own custom plague types, worlds, and events, which can then be shared with the community. It is a creation tool, not a standalone game.

If you are just starting out, a common beginner strategy is to pick a poor, heavily populated country with busy airports and ports (like India or China) and focus early on Transmission and Abilities to spread silently before evolving lethal Symptoms.
